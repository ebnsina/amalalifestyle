# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- AMALA Lifestyle as an installable web app: add it to your home screen from the browser and it opens like any
  other app.
- Works offline after the first visit. Everything stays on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
- বাংলা and English: switch on the welcome screen or in Profile. Screens, foods, prayers, workouts, numbers and
  dates all follow your choice.
- Tasks with a date and priority (urgent, normal, can wait), and filters for today, overdue, upcoming, done and all.
  Overdue tasks are marked in red; home shows how many tasks are left today.
- Everything from the phone app: food log with suggested portions, prayer tracker with streak, fitness routines with
  a rest timer, reading tracker, BMI, favourites, history, and backup to a file. Phone app backups import here too.
- Add your own foods with their measure, calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber; edit or delete them later. A food
  you add while picking a meal is ticked for it straight away.
- Your own gym routines can record the weight (kg) for each exercise.
- More compact screens: search and the category filter share one line, and foods, prayers and routines show two
  or three to a row.
- Install button on Home and in Profile. It opens the browser's install dialog, or shows the steps on iPhone and
  other browsers.
- Type Bangla on an English keyboard, as in Avro: "ami bhalo achi" becomes "আমি ভালো আছি" as you finish each word.
  Turn it off in Profile to keep English words.
- Fitness: make your own routine with its exercises, sets and rest; edit or delete it later.
- 22 more foods, including শসা, টমেটো, গাজর, সালাদ, ওটস, পাউরুটি, ছোলা, ফল, বাদাম and নুডলস.

### Fixed
- Install: on Chrome the Home card waits until Chrome can install, so the button always works; Profile explains the
  30-second wait. An already installed app no longer offers itself again.
- The browser no longer machine-translates the app, which garbled food names; use the app's own English instead.
- The on-screen keyboard no longer covers the field you're typing in or the buttons below it.

- Every tool has its own date range: prayer shows how many days you prayed each prayer and kept all five, fitness
  shows active days, minutes and routines, reading shows pages read per book, and tasks filter by due date.
- Reading now keeps a daily log of pages moved forward, so the totals cover any dates from now on.
- When a new version is out, a banner offers to switch to it in one tap; your data stays as it is.
- Past days: filter by a date range, with quick picks for the last 7 days, last 30 days and this month, and the
  average calories a day for the range.
- Tasks: pick the date from a calendar in your language.
- Food categories, task filters and the meal switcher sit on one line that scrolls sideways, instead of wrapping
  onto several rows.
- Books: a clear Save button for the page you typed, with a "Saved" note, and a tidier card.
- Prayers, tasks and foods: tap anywhere on a row to tick it, not just the small box.
- Prayer week view: witr and nafl now have their own rows, every row is labelled with the prayer, and today is
  highlighted.
- Adding a task or a book opens a sheet from the bottom instead of a form that is always on screen; action
  buttons sit on the right.
- Light and dark themes: follow your phone, or pick light or dark in Profile.

### Changed
- New name and brand: AMALA — "Purify Your Life, Transform Your Future."
- Type: Noto Serif Bengali for Bangla; Bricolage Grotesque for headings and text, Geist Mono for numbers in English.
- Book pages: one field to type the page or step it up and down.
