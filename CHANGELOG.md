# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- AMALA Lifestyle as an installable web app: add it to your home screen from the browser and it opens like any
  other app.
- Works offline after the first visit. Everything stays on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
- বাংলা and English: switch on the welcome screen or in Profile. Screens, foods, prayers, workouts, numbers and
  dates all follow your choice.
- Tasks with a date and priority (urgent, normal, can wait), and filters for today, overdue, upcoming, done and all.
  Overdue tasks are marked in red; home shows how many tasks are left today.
- Everything from the phone app: food log with suggested portions, prayer tracker with streak, fitness routines with
  a rest timer, reading tracker, BMI, favourites, history, and backup to a file. Phone app backups import here too.
- Past days: filter by a date range, with quick picks for the last 7 days, last 30 days and this month, and the
  average calories a day for the range.
- Tasks: pick the date from a calendar in your language.
- Food categories, task filters and the meal switcher sit on one line that scrolls sideways, instead of wrapping
  onto several rows.
- Books: a clear Save button for the page you typed, with a "Saved" note, and a tidier card.
- Prayers, tasks and foods: tap anywhere on a row to tick it, not just the small box.
- Prayer week view: witr and nafl now have their own rows, every row is labelled with the prayer, and today is
  highlighted.
- Adding a task or a book opens a sheet from the bottom instead of a form that is always on screen; action
  buttons sit on the right.
- Light and dark themes: follow your phone, or pick light or dark in Profile.

### Changed
- New name and brand: AMALA — "Purify Your Life, Transform Your Future."
- Type: Noto Serif Bengali for Bangla; Clash Display for headings, Inter Tight for text and Geist Mono for numbers in English.
- Book pages: one field to type the page or step it up and down.
